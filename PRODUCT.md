# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Chosen per experiment. The default is plain HTML, CSS and JS in the experiment folder, with no build step.

## Users

Tejas Page, a software engineer who builds and judges the experiments. Other people see some experiments as showcase recordings and screenshots. When an experiment has its own audience, the request that starts it describes them.

## Product Purpose

A personal sandbox for UI/UX, visualization and design experiments. Each experiment lives in its own `experiments/<slug>/` folder. The sandbox serves three purposes. It evaluates design skills and tools, such as Impeccable and Emil Kowalski's skills. It builds Tejas's design judgment. It produces portfolio pieces.

## Operating Context

Coding agents in T3 Code (Cursor and Codex) build the experiments, with Impeccable 4.8.0 installed for both. The repo is local only. Until it gets a remote, experiments are shown through screen recordings and screenshots.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Subjects are fictional or personal. Nothing in the sandbox relates to my employer or its work.
- Fonts, images and icons must be licensed for public use, since recordings and screenshots of any experiment may be shared.

## Evidence on Hand

None. The sandbox has no real users, customers, testimonials or metrics. Experiments may use invented data but must never present it as real.

## Product Principles

- Each experiment is self-contained in its own folder.
- Invented content is fine. Presenting it as real is not.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No sandbox-wide standard. Each experiment sets its own.
