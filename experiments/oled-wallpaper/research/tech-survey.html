Web graphics options for a static OLED wallpaper, grouped by layer. The pill rates fit for this job: one still image, rendered once at native resolution, where most pixels must be exact black.

Target. The OLED MacBook Pro ships Nov 6, 2026: 3024 × 1986 (14") or 3472 × 2266 (16"), with a camera cutout and Dynamic Island at the top centre.
Output. macOS wallpaper takes PNG, HEIC, JPEG or TIFF. Users report HDR files showing as SDR, so plan for an 8-bit SDR PNG.
Main risk. Near-black falloff bands badly at 8 bits. The fix is to render in float and dither at the final quantise.

GPU APIs

WebGL 2strong
Runs everywhere. Float render targets via EXT_color_buffer_float. Plenty for a fragment-shader render.
WebGPUpossible
Default in Chrome, Safari 26 and Firefox on Apple Silicon. Compute shaders help particle or path-traced work. HDR canvas is moot for an SDR file.

3D libraries and engines

three.js r187strong
WebGLRenderer, or WebGPURenderer with TSL node shaders. Pick it if the piece is a lit 3D scene.
three-gpu-pathtracerstrong
Accumulates samples over seconds or minutes. Too slow for live, which doesn't matter for a still. Real soft light falling into black.
OGL, twgl, reglpossible
Thin helpers, 28 to 39 KB. Less boilerplate than raw WebGL. OGL and regl haven't shipped since 2025, regl is WebGL 1 only.
Babylon.js, PlayCanvaspoor
Full game engines, 0.6 to 1.8 MB. Their editors and physics do nothing for one still image.

Shader-first tools

Raw fragment shaderstrong
Shadertoy style: one shader per pixel, SDF raymarching or fields. Full control over every black pixel.
Lygiastrong
Shader function library in GLSL and WGSL: noise, SDFs, colour, dithering. Pairs with any option here.
Shader Parkpossible
SDF shapes from a p5-like JS API, no GLSL. Fast to sketch with, but less control over the final output.
Hydrapoor
Live-coded video synth for performance. Its whole point is motion.

2D and vector

SVGpossible
Crisp at any size, exact #000 for free. Weak at glow and soft light, and its filters band.
Canvas 2D, p5.jspossible
Good for line work and generative patterns. CPU-bound, and gradients are 8-bit.
PixiJSpoor
2D sprite renderer for games. Nothing here needs sprites.

Export to a file

toBlob PNG + ditherstrong
Render to a float target, dither once into 8-bit, save. The simplest path to a wallpaper file.
16-bit PNG masterpossible
readPixels from the float target, encoded in JS. A lossless master to regrade later. macOS shows it at 8 bits.
Headless renderpossible
Playwright drives the page and saves the file. Repeatable seeds and sizes from one command. More setup than a button.
